OKX to list perpetual futures for QNT equity

Published on 5 Jun 20262 min read
This information may not apply to all customers

This is a general update. Please check the availability of the listed token or product for your region in the app.

We are pleased to announce that USDT-margined perpetual futures for selected stocks will be enabled from 10:30 UTC on June 5, 2026. For product details, refer to our stock perpetual futures documentation. The updates will cover both the web and app interfaces as well as the API. The details are as follows:


I. Listing schedule
